Su'aalaha caadiga ah

Apps-kaygu ma furmin kadib socodka.

Hubi in Socod uu leeyahay ogolaanshaha Motion & Fitness: Dejinta iPhone-ka → Privacy & Security → Motion & Fitness → Socod. Tallaabooyinku way tirmaan xataa marka Socod xiran yahay. Apps-kaagu marwalba way furmaan dhammaadka wakhtiga qufulka.

Daqiiqadaha jimicsiga lama tirin.

Habka jimicsigu wuxuu tiriyaa jimicsiga guriga ee app-ka ku jira, ama daqiiqadaha jimicsiga ee Apple Watch ama app jimicsi oo u qora Apple Health. U ogolow Socod inuu akhriyo Exercise Minutes: app-ka Health → astaantaada → Apps → Socod.

Sideen u tijaabiyaa qufulka anigoon sugin subaxa?

Socod → Dejinta → Waqtiga qufulka → Tijaabi qufulka hadda. Wuxuu xiraa apps-ka aad dooratay 15 daqiiqo; waad joojin kartaa ka hor.

Socod ma xiri karaa wicitaannada?

Maya. Wicitaannada iyo xaaladaha degdegga ah marwalba way shaqeeyaan.

Sideen u joojiyaa Premium?

Dejinta iPhone-ka → magacaaga → Subscriptions → Socod, ama Socod → Dejinta → Premium → Maamul rukhsadda. Tirtiridda app-ku ma joojiso rukhsadda.

Premium waxaan ku iibsaday iPhone kale.

Socod → Dejinta → Premium → Soo celi iibsashada (isla Apple ID-ga).

Sideen u tirtiraa xogtayda?

Socod → Dejinta → Asturnaanta → Tirtir xogtayda la kaydiyay. Halkaas ayaad sidoo kale ka dami kartaa kaydinta. Eeg Siyaasadda Asturnaanta.

Sideen gebi ahaanba u joojiyaa Socod?

Tirtir app-ka. Dhammaan qufulladiisa isla markiiba way baxaan.
