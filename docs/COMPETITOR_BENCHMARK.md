# Competitor benchmark: launch sites (30 Oct 2026)

A short pass over the sites of the leading screen-time and step apps. Some pages could not be fetched
(Opal, Sweatcoin, StepBet, WeWard); for those, the notes below come from store and third-party listings.

| App | What converts | Where it falls short |
|---|---|---|
| Opal | "#1 Screen Time app" positioning, big user and team claims | No visible pricing; Facebook Pixel and Google Analytics on the site |
| one sec | Peer-reviewed study, press logos, strong privacy message | Premium price never shown; Japanese page mixes in English and raw code text |
| ScreenZen | "No subscription" hero, 5-step how-it-works, short FAQ | Conflicting user counts, duplicated reviews, self-reported headline stat |
| Brick | 3-step how-it-works, clear product prices, money-back line | Unsourced review count and survey stats; no emergency or data-handling answer |
| StepBet | Simple "bet, walk, win" promise | Money rules need explaining; site could not be reviewed directly |
| Sweatcoin | "Get paid to walk" promise | Tiers and fees unclear in public write-ups; GPS and data questions unanswered |
| WeWard | Rewards and charity story | No pricing, no data or privacy explanation on its store page |

## What the Socod page does (no fake reviews, testimonials or invented stats)

1. **Prices on the page.** Free plan, Premium at $0.99/month or $4.99/year, trial terms and how to cancel.
2. **Privacy where people decide.** No sign-up, no ads, no tracking, and what stays on the iPhone. The site itself has no analytics, cookies or third-party scripts.
3. **Safety answered up front.** Calls and emergencies always work, and the lock always ends.
4. **Full Somali, English and Arabic.** Each language is a whole page (Arabic is right to left), never mixed.
5. **Fast and light.** No web fonts, one small stylesheet, a 5 KB icon and a tiny optional script. The page works without JavaScript.

Hero, 3-step how-it-works, pricing, FAQ, call to action. The layout is built for a 390 px phone first.
