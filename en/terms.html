What Socod is

Socod helps you build a habit: it locks apps you choose during a time you choose, until you finish a walk or a workout. It is not a medical device and does not give medical advice. Talk to a doctor before starting a new exercise routine, and always walk somewhere safe and watch for traffic.

The lock

  • You choose which apps are locked and when. Phone calls and emergency features are never blocked.
  • The lock always ends at the end of the window you set, even if you did not walk.
  • Deleting Socod removes all its locks straight away.
  • Socod is not responsible for messages or notifications you see later because an app you chose was locked.

The Wallahi oath

The oath unlock is optional and limited each week. It is a personal matter between you and Allah; Socod only records how often it was used, for your own honesty log.

Socod Premium (auto-renewing subscription)

The core of Socod is free. Socod Premium adds all challenges, the 30-day history and exclusive badges.

  • Plans: Socod Premium Monthly (renews every month) and Socod Premium Yearly (renews every year). The price for your country is shown in the app before you buy. In the United States the launch prices are $0.99 per month and $4.99 per year.
  • Free trial: the yearly plan may include a 1-month free trial if you are eligible. If you do not cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, the yearly price is charged. Socod reminds you 2 days before a trial ends. Any unused part of a free trial ends when you buy a subscription.
  • Payment is charged to your Apple ID account when you confirm the purchase.
  • Automatic renewal: the subscription renews automatically for the same length and price unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for renewal within the 24 hours before the current period ends.
  • Manage or cancel at any time in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions, or in Socod: Settings → Premium → Manage subscription. Cancelling stops the next renewal; Premium stays until the end of the period you paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple under Apple's rules, at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Price changes follow Apple's rules, and Apple tells you before they apply.
