Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Socod collects as little as possible. There is no sign-up, no email, no ads and no tracking, and we never sell data.

Socod is made by Mohamed Abdullahi Kassim, an independent developer (app studio: SoMeal Technologies). Questions about privacy: myccharlotte+socod@gmail.com.

What stays only on your iPhone

  • The apps you lock and your lock times. Socod uses Apple's Screen Time tools. iOS gives Socod only coded tokens for the apps you pick, not their names, and Socod cannot see what you do inside any app.
  • Apple Health data. With your permission Socod reads exercise minutes (to unlock after a workout) and active calories (to show your progress). This data is never uploaded and never shared. Socod never writes to Apple Health.
  • Live step counting from the iPhone's motion sensor, your settings, reminders and your honesty log.

What is backed up, and why

So that your progress is kept and family and friend groups work, Socod backs up the following. This is on by default and you can turn it off at any time in Settings → Privacy → Back up my progress and use groups. When it is off, Socod sends nothing to our server.

  • An anonymous account ID created automatically for your phone (no name, email or phone number).
  • For each day: total steps, glasses of water, whether you finished your walk or workout, and whether you unlocked with the oath.
  • Overall progress: XP, number of walks, oaths used, total steps and the start date of your Walk to Makkah journey.
  • Your app language, walk or workout mode, and that you use iPhone.
  • The display name you type for groups (or "Socod" if you leave it empty), and the groups you create or join.

Who can see it: only you. People in a group you join see your display name, your steps this week and your number of walks.

What it is used for: only to run Socod (backup, groups and the weekly group board). Never for advertising, never for tracking you across other apps or websites, never sold or shared with data brokers.

Companies that help us

  • Supabase hosts Socod's database. Like any web service, its servers may keep short technical logs (such as IP address) for security and to keep the service running.
  • Apple handles App Store purchases. We never receive your card or payment details; your iPhone only tells Socod whether Premium is active.

Keeping and deleting your data

Backed-up data is kept while backup is on. You can delete it yourself at any time: Settings → Privacy → Delete my backed-up data. This removes your backed-up history, progress, group memberships and the anonymous account from our server, and turns backup off. You can also write to myccharlotte+socod@gmail.com with the word "Delete".

Deleting the Socod app removes everything stored on your phone, but not the backup. Use the delete button first if you want both gone.

Your choices and rights

You can turn off backup, refuse or remove Motion & Fitness, Apple Health and notification access in iPhone Settings, and ask us to see, correct or delete your data by writing to myccharlotte+socod@gmail.com.

Children

Socod is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date at the top. Important changes are also mentioned in the app's release notes.
