Socod locks the apps you choose, at the time you choose, until you finish a walk or a workout. Made for Muslims, in Somali, English and Arabic.

How it works

  1. Pick your apps and your time

    Choose the apps that take over your mornings and the window they stay locked, up to 6 hours.

  2. Walk or work out

    Socod counts your steps live from the iPhone's motion sensor, or exercise minutes from Apple Health and the built-in home workout.

  3. Your apps open

    Reach your goal and they unlock. Even if you don't, the lock always ends when your window ends.

Questions

Can Socod block calls or emergencies?

No. Phone calls and emergency features always work.

What if I can't walk today?

The lock always ends at the end of the window you set, whether you walked or not.

What does Socod do with my data?

There is no sign-up, no ads and no tracking, and we never sell data. The apps you lock and your Apple Health data stay on your iPhone. Backup of your progress for groups is optional: you can turn it off or delete it in Settings. Privacy Policy

Do I need an Apple Watch?

No. Your iPhone counts your steps. Workout minutes from Apple Watch or a gym app that writes to Apple Health count too.

How do I cancel Premium?

iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Socod. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Is Socod available in my country?

Socod is on the App Store in most countries outside the European Union. There is no App Store for Somalia, so if you live in Somalia you need an Apple ID from a country where the App Store is available. If you get stuck, write to us and we will gladly help.
